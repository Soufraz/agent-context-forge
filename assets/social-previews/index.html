📸 How to upload to GitHub:
  1. Pick the design option you like best above.
  2. Take a screenshot of the 1280×640 image frame (macOS: Cmd + Shift + 4, spacebar, click the frame; or right-click the image and save).
  3. Go to your GitHub repository: Settings → General → Social preview.
  4. Click Edit → Upload an image and select your saved image!
