📸 How to upload to GitHub:
  1. Click between the 3 tabs above to choose your favorite look.
  2. Screenshot option: Take a screenshot of the 1280×640 image frame (on Mac: press Cmd + Shift + 4, then Spacebar, and click the image frame).
  3. Or open raw: Click "Open Raw SVG in New Tab" to view or save the standalone file.
  4. Go to your repository: Settings → General → Social preview (or github.com/Soufraz/agent-context-forge/settings).
  5. Click Edit → Upload an image and select your file!
